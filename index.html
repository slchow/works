About Me

I have a master's degree in English and Rhetoric and an undergraduate degree in English with a minor in Editing, both from Brigham Young University. I've worked in communications for over fifteen years — as an editor at academic and trade publishers, and as a writer and marketer in the tech sector.

As an editor, I'm passionate about realizing the potential in others' writing. Everyone should feel confident their writing will be understood as intended, and no great idea should be cloaked by stilted prose. On the side, I've helped a number of friends and family self-publish their life stories for their loved ones.

Your life story is a gift to the future. The effort you put into writing your personal history will be appreciated more than you know, and I love helping people print their stories so they can be preserved and shared.

"No great ideas should be cloaked by stilted writing. Everyone deserves to feel confident their story will be understood exactly as intended."
